A way into the dark

DiggerChain is a game for your terminal. You are a human delver exploring a castle hidden beneath a forest. Break through veins of rock, gather crystals, and look for a portal to the floor below.

The alpha expedition has three floors and a limit of 512 successful actions. There is no timer rushing you. You can return to earlier floors, and the things you collect stay collected. The seemingly endless castle belongs to the story; these are the current playable limits.

Want to get a feel for it? Play the browser demo. It uses the same expedition rules, with a browser view. It is practice only, without a wallet or rewards.

Get the terminal game

Choose your platform below. The Linux build supports Ubuntu 22.04 or compatible systems. Pick the portable Windows build or the macOS version matching your processor.

{{DOWNLOADS}}

Extract the ZIP and open a terminal in that folder. On Linux or macOS, run:

chmod +x diggerchain
./diggerchain

On Windows, run .\diggerchain.exe in PowerShell. Use a terminal with full color and at least 90 columns by 30 rows. You do not need Rust, Node.js, a wallet, or an internet connection to play the downloaded game.

The browser demo supports movement, E, and F, plus on-screen controls. G, zoom, and persistent saves belong to the terminal game.

Spend your charge wisely

You start with 8 charge and can carry up to 12. Soft rock costs 2 to break; hard rock costs 3. Ordinary walls cannot be mined.

A flask restores up to 8 charge. Stand on it and press E. A used flask is gone for that expedition, even if you leave the floor and come back. At full charge, the flask stays available.

Walking and using portals cost no charge. If you run empty, retrace your steps and look for an unused flask. Waiting does not refill anything.

The deeper light

Walk onto a crystal to collect it. A crystal on the first floor is worth 1 score, the second floor 4, and the third floor 16. Deeper crystals are scarcer.

Find a downward portal and press E to descend. The upward portal takes you back. There is no requirement to clear a floor or collect every crystal before leaving.

A seed determines the expedition: rock hardness, some passages, and crystal positions vary inside the current chamber layout. The first release uses one castle setting. More varied layouts, biomes, and deeper expeditions are future work.

Unexplored areas stay dark. Finding the passage is part of the expedition.

Leave a way back

The terminal game saves every successful action. Press Q and launch it again to continue the same expedition. Your mined passages, remaining charge, and collected items are kept.

Press F when you are ready to finish. You can finish before reaching the deepest floor. To start a fresh expedition afterward:

./diggerchain --new

The previous save is archived. For a repeatable practice world, choose a seed:

./diggerchain --new --seed 42

An explicit seed creates a repeatable practice attempt. Alpha does not pay tokens. Native saves belong to your user account's application-data folder. Back them up privately: a save contains your expedition's secret. Do not post it in a public issue.

The browser demo lasts while this page stays open. Switching site sections keeps the attempt; reloading the page or choosing New descent starts over.

Give your agent a lantern

An external agent can play the downloaded game through its text interface. Start it with:

./diggerchain --headless

The game accepts one command per line and replies with JSON. Your agent can look around, move, dig, use flasks, travel through portals, save, and finish. Movement and digging are separate commands in this mode.

Bring your own agent and its model connection. DiggerChain does not include an AI subscription or ask for your AI account credentials. Headless play follows the same rules as manual play.

From alpha to rewards

Alpha is for playing and exploring. It has no direct $DCG or RWA-token payouts. Your crystal score is a game result, not a token balance or an active points balance.

Phase II brings the planned beta points system for a future airdrop. More details will arrive with that phase.

Direct $DCG rewards together with supported RWA-token fractions are planned for Phase III, the full release. NVDA, AAPL and MSFT are examples of intended token integrations; supported assets and access still need to be confirmed.

You can play the native game offline. No wallet or reward submission is required for ordinary alpha play. Earlier proof and testnet experiments remain development work, not a live alpha claim flow.
